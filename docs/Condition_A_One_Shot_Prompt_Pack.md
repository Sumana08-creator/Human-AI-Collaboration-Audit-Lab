## Shared instruction
You are assisting with a fictional healthcare governance exercise. Use only the source evidence supplied below. Do not invent facts, dates, policies, causes, or outcomes. Distinguish evidence from inference and recommendation. If evidence is insufficient, say so explicitly. For each material factual claim, identify the source fact that supports it. Do not provide clinical diagnosis or treatment advice.

No risk scores or incident counts are provided. Prepare a concise governance review containing key findings, existing controls, missing information and limitations, clearly labelled recommendations, and a short evidence-based conclusion.
